# Privacy Policy for Laundry Loop

**Effective date:** March 25, 2026

Laundry Loop (the "App") is a local-first Apple Watch app with widget support. This Privacy Policy explains what information the App processes and how that information is handled.

## 1. Data We Process

Laundry Loop does **not** require account creation and does **not** collect personal profile information.

The App processes and stores the following data **on your device only**:

- **App settings** (such as default washer duration, default dryer duration, reminder lead time, and haptics preference)
- **Recent cycle presets** (such as cycle type, duration, and last-used timestamp)
- **Active cycle snapshot data** (such as timer state and timestamps needed to run, restore, and display your active cycle)

To support widgets, relevant timer snapshot data is stored in an Apple app group container shared between the Watch app and its widget extension.

## 2. Permissions

Laundry Loop may request permission to send **local notifications** so you can receive cycle completion and reminder alerts.

- Notification permission is optional.
- You can enable or disable notifications at any time in your Apple device settings.

Laundry Loop does not request permissions for contacts, location, camera, microphone, photos, or advertising tracking.

## 3. Data Sharing, Tracking, and Third Parties

Laundry Loop is designed to run locally on device.

- No personal account is required.
- No cloud sync is used.
- No backend service is used to process your app data.
- No third-party analytics SDKs or ad SDKs are used for tracking your behavior.
- Laundry Loop does not use App Tracking Transparency (ATT) to track you across apps or websites.
- Laundry Loop does not use IDFA for advertising or cross-app tracking.
- We do not sell or share personal data with data brokers or advertisers.

## 4. Data Retention and Your Controls

Because Laundry Loop stores data locally, your data remains on your device unless you remove it.

You can control your data by:

- disabling notifications in system settings, and/or
- removing app data by uninstalling the app (and associated widget/app data, as managed by Apple platform behavior).

## 5. Children’s Privacy

Laundry Loop is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## 6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect app updates, legal requirements, or operational changes. When we update this policy, we will revise the "Effective date" at the top.
